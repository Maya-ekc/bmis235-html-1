Maya's Top Five Reads

  • ONE: Anne of Green Gables, by Lucy M Montgomery: Though I think that the book is marketed towards children, I think that is a beautiful read for anyone, of any age. The first book is what brough about my love for Anne at a young age, and as a series the Montgomery builds a wonderous world that combines love, friendship, hardships, and hope culminating into what was my first ever favorite book. Obviously it is a childrens book, at a lower reading level, but I don't that makes it any less worthy to be read.
  • TWO: The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo, by Taylor Jenkins Reid: To be honest, I think that I would recommend any book by Taylor Jenkins Reid, she's one of my favorite authors, however TSHOEH just happened to be the first book of hers I read, and I was immediately hooked. This is less of a romance in my opinion, it's slight mystery, and a lot of drama, so I think that's what I would categorize it as.
  • THREE: The Song of Achilles, by Madeline Miller: Admitidly, I took a lomg time to pick this book up becuase it seemed so overhyped for a long time, but once I read it I loved it. There is a lot of true emotions laced into the book, and it takes a certain kind of book to make me cry, and this one did. It's a beautiful story, and I think reading Circe after this one would be perfect, so basically a two in one. i would say read if you enjoy love, but also obviously mythology.
  • FOUR: The Tattooist of Auschwitz, by Heather Morris: This book has been one that I tend to reread often, but I won't lie when I say it is a hard read. I'm not necessarily someone who tends to read autobiographies or biographies, or really anything adjacent to any sort of life story, but the Tattooist of Auschwitz is such a powerful story, and well worth reading.
  • FIVE: The Importance of Being Earnest, by Oscar Wilde: While not technically a book, I love The Importance of Being Earnest so much. I love Jane Austin and the Bronte Sisters, and I would recommend just about any of their books as well, but for some reason this story just sticks with me. There is chaos, love, issues of rank and family problems, all in one short play and pretty much surrounding just four people, and I'd say it's highly enjoyable drama.
